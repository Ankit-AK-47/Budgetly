# 💰 Monthly Expense & Budget Tracker

A simple and user-friendly app designed to help you manage your monthly finances, track expenses, and stay within your budget.

## 🚀 Features

- 💵 **Income Tracking** — Record your monthly income.
- 🧾 **Expense Management** — Add and categorize daily expenses.
- 📊 **Budget Planning** — Set monthly budgets and spending limits.
- 💰 **Savings Tracker** — Calculate how much money you save each month.
- 📈 **Spending Analysis** — Understand where your money goes.
- 🎯 **Financial Goals** — Build better money management habits.

## 🎯 Purpose

The goal of this app is to make personal finance management simple, helping users control spending, avoid unnecessary expenses, and improve their saving habits.

## 🛠️ Getting Started

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Follow the setup instructions for the project.
3. Launch the app and enter your income, budget, and expenses.
4. Track your spending and review your monthly savings.
